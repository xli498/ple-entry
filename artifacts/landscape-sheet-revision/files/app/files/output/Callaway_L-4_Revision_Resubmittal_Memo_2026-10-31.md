Recommendation

We should not commit to November 30 as an unconditional date today. The date is achievable as a working target, but only if Callaway authorizes the revised frontage planting scope immediately and the design team closes the listed dependencies on the dates below. The current L-set is Revision 0, dated August 21, 2026, and does not satisfy the L-4 comment.

The controlling landscape issue is substantive, not clerical. The County's L-4 comment requires one tree per 20 linear feet for 4,160 feet of frontage: 208 street-frontage trees. The current L-2 plan and L-1 schedule show 58 street-frontage trees, leaving 150 additional street trees to be designed, coordinated, counted, and shown. The entry and park trees are separate from that frontage count.

The existing irrigation documents are based on 74 total trees and report 3.28 acre-feet per year: 58 street trees, 6 entry trees, and 10 park trees. If the same 2,000-gallon-per-tree allowance is retained, the additional 150 street trees are approximately 300,000 gallons per year, or 0.92 acre-feet per year. That produces a preliminary planning total of approximately 4.20 acre-feet per year. This is not a substitute for Ocotillo's revised calculation; the final number must be recalculated against the actual species, locations, hydrozones, emitter/bubbler circuits, and revised sheets.
