1. OBSERVATION
On 07/01/2026 during routine walk-through, water pooling was observed at drain C-4 (Zone 1 low point,
drain invert El. 734.5). Estimated seepage rate 2–3 gpm.
At time of observation the basement was otherwise dry. The daily field record logs 0.42 in of rain
overnight on 06/30-07/01. The slab here lies about 15 ft below grade with no surface path to this point;
ambient temperature at the walk-through was approximately 78°F.
Standing water at the drain covered an area of approximately 3 ft × 4 ft on the slab surface. The pooling had
migrated to the drain grate from the immediately surrounding slab; no active surface flow from adjacent
penetrations, sleeves, or piping was observed. Slab surface adjacent to the drain was noticeably darker than
the surrounding dry field, indicating extended wetness prior to the walk-through.
A hairline crack in the slab was noted adjacent to drain C-4, running generally parallel to the nearest column
line for a length of approximately 40 linear feet centered on the drain. The crack appears tight but is damp
along its full observed length. A small wet patch was also noted on the interior face of the basement wall
approximately 6 feet from C-4, at low elevation (within 12 inches of the slab).
Zone 1 finished floor slopes at 1% fall from the perimeter toward the C-4 drain across grids B through G. The
drain invert (El. 734.5) is the low point of the Zone 1 pocket.

3. DIAGNOSIS
Water appears to enter through a hairline crack in slab adjacent to drain C-4. Recommend polyurethane
crack injection for approximately 40 linear feet centered on C-4.
The recommended repair method is a hydrophobic polyurethane injection resin, injected under low pressure
through surface ports installed at nominal 12-inch centers along the observed crack. The resin is intended to
fill the crack aperture full-depth and cure into a flexible seal. The method is standard practice for the
observed defect geometry and is compatible with the existing slab.

Scope
Polyurethane crack injection of the hairline crack in the basement slab in the vicinity of drain C-4 for a
nominal length of 40 linear feet, including:

•   Surface preparation (grinding, degreasing) of the crack alignment;

•   Installation of surface ports at approximately 12-inch centers;

•   Low-pressure injection of hydrophobic polyurethane resin to refusal;

•   Removal of ports and grinding of surface residue flush with slab;

•   Cleanup and disposal of consumables.
