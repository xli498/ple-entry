       ORDVALE BUILDING PRODUCTS, INC. — PRODUCT DATA SHEET
       AQUASTOP HD
       Positive-Side Bentonite Composite Waterproofing Membrane
       Product Data Sheet — Revision 08, Effective 09/01/2025

       Description: Aquastop HD is a factory-laminated composite waterproofing membrane consisting of a granular sodium bentonite core
       encapsulated between a high-strength woven polypropylene geotextile carrier and a non-woven polypropylene protection layer. On contact
       with water, the bentonite core swells within the confined space between the geotextiles to form a low-permeability seal that arrests moisture
       and pressurized groundwater. Aquastop HD is intended for positive-side (soil-side) application to concrete substrates below grade, including
       horizontal blindside, vertical wall and wall-to-mat construction joint conditions where hydrostatic pressure is present.

       Composition:
 Component                                      Specification

 Core                                           Sodium bentonite, 1.0 lb/ft² min. dry weight

 Upper carrier                                  Woven polypropylene geotextile, 200 g/m²

 Lower protection                               Non-woven polypropylene geotextile, 200 g/m²

 Nominal thickness                              0.24 in (6.1 mm)

 Roll size                                      4 ft x 15 ft, 60 ft²

 Weight                                         1.2 lb/ft²

       Performance Data:
 Property                                            Test Method                         Value

 Hydrostatic head rating                             ASTM D5385 modified                 Rated to 20 ft head (60 ft head at failure)

       Application Limits and Conditions of Use:
       • Intended for use under continuous or intermittent hydrostatic pressure up to the rated head. Suitable for wall-to-mat construction joints,
           blindside walls against soldier-pile lagging, elevator pits, and slabs cast on prepared subgrade.
       • Substrate concrete shall be cured a minimum of 5 days prior to application.
       • Compatible hydrophilic waterstop (Aquastop CB profile) shall be installed continuously across all construction joints in conjunction with the
           sheet membrane.
       • Membrane shall be protected from ponded standing water prior to backfill or slab pour. Once encapsulated by the second concrete lift or
           protection course, the membrane is activated by ground moisture and its performance is not impaired by prior wetting.

       Approved Compatible Accessories: Aquastop CB reinforcing fabric (12 in x 100 ft rolls); Aquastop HydroBar hydrophilic waterstop;
       Aquastop Mastic sealant for penetrations.

       ORDVALE BUILDING PRODUCTS, INC. — PRODUCT DATA SHEET
       BITUMEX 200
       Cold-Applied Bituminous Damp-Proofing Coating
       Product Data Sheet — Revision 05, Effective 07/15/2025

       Description: Bitumex 200 is a cold-applied, single-component asphalt-emulsion damp-proofing coating for below-grade concrete and
       masonry surfaces. It provides a continuous moisture-resistant barrier for above-water-table below-grade conditions where the wall or slab is
       exposed only to soil moisture, capillary rise or intermittent surface percolation. Bitumex 200 is spray, brush or roller applied and is normally
       installed in two coats.

       Composition:
 Component                                           Specification

 Type                                                Cold-applied asphalt emulsion, water-based

 Solids by weight                                    55 ± 3%

 Coverage                                            40 ft²/gal per coat

 Nominal dry film thickness                          30 mil per coat (60 mil total, two coats)

 Package                                             5 gal pail; 55 gal drum

       Performance Data:

 Surface burning characteristics (cured film)            ASTM E84                                Flame spread index 15, smoke developed index 90 (Class A)

 Cure to recoat                                          Mfr. procedure                          24 hr @ 70°F, 50% RH

 Cure to backfill                                        Mfr. procedure                          72 hr @ 70°F, 50% RH

 Service temperature                                     —                                       −20°F to +160°F

       Rating and Conditions of Use:
       BITUMEX 200 IS RATED FOR NON-HYDROSTATIC CONDITIONS ONLY.

       This product is a damp-proofing coating within the meaning of IBC Section 1805.2. It is intended for use on the exterior of
       foundation walls and slabs where the finished exterior grade sits above the seasonal high groundwater elevation and where the
       wall or slab is not subject to standing water or hydrostatic pressure. Bitumex 200 shall not be substituted for a positive-side
       hydrostatic waterproofing membrane in locations where the design groundwater elevation is at or above the elevation of the coated
       surface.

       • Compatible with non-swelling PVC waterstops of center-bulb or split-flange type at construction joints.
       • Substrate concrete shall be cured a minimum of 5 days prior to application; surface shall be dry to the touch and free of frost, loose material
          and form-release residue.
       • Second coat shall be applied after first coat has cured to a tack-free state (typ. 24 hr @ 70°F). Do not apply below 40°F or if rain is forecast
          within 12 hr of application.

       Approved Compatible Accessories: Standard-profile non-swelling PVC waterstops (6 in or 9 in center-bulb, thermally welded);
       polyethylene protection board for backfill contact areas.
